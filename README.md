Uzbek Medical Studio

AI-assisted medical education in Uzbek.

Uzbek Medical Studio is an open-source initiative focused on making medical education more accessible to Uzbek-speaking students and learners.

Our Mission

To simplify complex medical concepts through accurate scientific information, 3D medical visualizations, educational videos, and AI-assisted content development.

Project Components

* Medical Knowledge: Structured, evidence-based medical educational content.
* 3D Visualization: Visual explanations of anatomy, physiology, pathology, and clinical procedures.
* AI-Assisted Production: Tools and workflows for creating educational content.
* Narration & Subtitles: Clear Uzbek-language narration and subtitles.
* Documentation: Reusable resources and production guidelines.

Target Audience

* Medical university students
* Healthcare learners
* Medical educators
* Uzbek-speaking communities interested in medical science

Project Status

Currently in development. Contributions, feedback, and collaboration are welcome.
